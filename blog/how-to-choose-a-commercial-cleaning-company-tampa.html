How to Choose a Commercial Cleaning Company in Tampa

Seven questions to ask before you sign with a cleaner, and the red flags most businesses only notice after it's too late.

October 1, 2026 · The Clean Standard

Most Tampa Bay businesses don't hire a new cleaning company because their last one was too expensive. They switch because of missed visits, a rotating crew, and texts that go unanswered. Here's what to ask before you sign with anyone.

1. Who will actually be cleaning my space?

Big companies often send whoever is available that night. Ask whether you'll see the same person each visit, and who you contact when something is missed.

2. Do you use a written checklist?

A good cleaner builds a checklist for your specific space after a walkthrough. If a company quotes you over the phone without seeing the space, expect surprises.

3. How do I reach you, and how fast do you respond?

Ask for a direct phone number. A 24-hour response window should be the minimum.

4. Are you insured?

Ask for a certificate of insurance and check that it's current. Most property managers and landlords require one.

5. What happens if something gets missed?

The right answer is "we come back and fix it free." Anything else means you'll be the one chasing them.

6. Is there a long-term contract?

Month-to-month service with a short notice period is common. Be careful of 12-month agreements with cancellation fees.

7. What's included, and what costs extra?

Get the scope in writing: restrooms, break rooms, glass, trash, floors, and restocking. Ask how add-ons like carpet care or interior glass are priced.
