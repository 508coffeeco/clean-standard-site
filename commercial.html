What's Included

Every facility customized. Nothing skipped.

Every commercial agreement starts with a walkthrough. Each facility is tailored, but core service typically includes:

High-touch surface disinfection
Restroom sanitation & restocking
Trash removal & liner replacement
Floor care (vacuum, mop, spot treatment)
Breakroom & kitchen cleaning
Entryway & reception detailing
Dusting of desks, surfaces & common areas
Interior glass cleaning (reachable)
Conference room resets
Supply restocking on request

Need more? We also offer deep commercial resets, after-hours service, event prep, and post-event cleanup as specialty add-ons.
