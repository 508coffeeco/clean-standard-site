The Airbnb Turnover Checklist St. Pete Hosts Actually Need

What a five-star turnover covers in a beach-town rental, including sand, humidity, and the lanai.

October 1, 2026 · The Clean Standard

Beach rentals in St. Pete, Treasure Island, and Madeira Beach have their own challenges: sand everywhere, humidity, and outdoor spaces guests actually use. Here's what every turnover should cover.

Bedrooms

  • Strip and remake beds with fresh linens
  • Check under beds and in drawers for left-behind items
  • Dust surfaces, lamps, and ceiling fan blades

Bathrooms

  • Scrub and disinfect the toilet, shower, tub, and sink
  • Check grout and caulk for mildew (humidity makes this a weekly issue in Florida)
  • Restock toilet paper, soap, and toiletries; stage fresh towels

Kitchen

  • Run and empty the dishwasher, then put everything away
  • Wipe appliances inside and out; clear the fridge of guest food
  • Restock coffee, paper towels, and dish soap

Beach-specific extras

  • Vacuum sand from entryways, rugs, and couch cushions
  • Rinse and stage beach chairs and toys
  • Sweep and wipe down the lanai or patio furniture
  • Run the AC dehumidify setting and check for musty smells

Before you leave

  • Take photos of every room
  • Report any damage or missing items to the host
  • Set the thermostat, lights, and locks for arrival
