Booking Policies

Scheduling, rescheduling, cancellations, and access.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Booking

Services are confirmed once you've accepted your quote and we've confirmed a date and time in writing by text or email.

Rescheduling and cancellations

Please give at least 48 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice may be charged 50% of the scheduled service. Short-term rental turnovers changed because of a guest booking change are rescheduled at no charge whenever possible.

Lockouts

If we can't access the space at the scheduled time and can't reach you within 15 minutes, the visit may be charged as a late cancellation.

Arrival windows

We arrive within the agreed window. Traffic and weather in Tampa Bay can cause delays; if we're running late, we'll text you.

Weather

During hurricanes, tropical storms, or other severe weather, we may reschedule for safety at no charge to you.

Holidays

Recurring services that fall on a major holiday are moved to the nearest available day.

Questions

Contact us at info@settingthecleanstandard.com or (508) 205-8810.
