Privacy Policy

How The Clean Standard collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Information we collect

When you request a quote, book a service, or contact us, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email, service address, business name, and details about your space. We may also collect basic, non-identifying website usage data such as pages visited and device type.

How we use it

  • To prepare quotes and schedule and perform services
  • To communicate with you by phone, text, or email about your service
  • To send invoices and process payments
  • To improve our website and services

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about quotes, scheduling, and your service. Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Message and data rates may apply.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (such as scheduling, payment, form, and email tools), or when required by law.

Access to your property

Any keys, codes, or access instructions you provide are used only to perform your service and are stored securely.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by contacting us.

Questions

Contact us at info@settingthecleanstandard.com or (508) 205-8810.
