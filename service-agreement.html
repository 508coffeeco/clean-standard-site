Service Agreement

What you can expect from us, and what we ask of you, on every service.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Our commitments

  • Arrive within the scheduled window and complete the agreed checklist
  • Use professional-grade supplies and equipment, or your preferred products on request
  • Respect your space, privacy, and security instructions
  • Communicate promptly about delays, issues, or anything we notice that needs attention
  • Return to fix any missed checklist item reported within 24 hours, free of charge

Your responsibilities

  • Provide safe access to the space at the scheduled time (keys, codes, alarm instructions)
  • Make sure running water and electricity are available
  • Secure valuables, cash, and fragile or sentimental items
  • Let us know about any hazards, pets, or special instructions in advance

Scope changes

Work outside the agreed checklist can be added at any time and will be quoted before it is performed.

Commercial accounts

Commercial service is month-to-month. Either party may end service with 14 days' written notice. Pricing is reviewed if your square footage, hours, or scope change.

Damage and claims

Please report any damage you believe was caused by our service within 24 hours, with photos. We will review every report and work with you on a fair resolution.

Safety

We may decline or pause service in conditions we consider unsafe, including biohazards, pest infestations, or aggressive animals.

Questions

Contact us at info@settingthecleanstandard.com or (508) 205-8810.
