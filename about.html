About

Where clean meets consistency.

The Clean Standard is an owner-operated cleaning company serving Tampa, St. Petersburg, and Clearwater.

Our Story

Hi, I'm Brenna.

I started The Clean Standard in Massachusetts, cleaning offices, short-term rentals, and homes on the South Shore. The work wasn't complicated, but I kept hearing the same complaint from new clients: their last cleaner was inconsistent, hard to reach, or stopped showing up.

So I built the business around the opposite. Written checklists for every space. Clear communication. The same standard on the fortieth visit as on the first.

Now I've brought The Clean Standard to Tampa Bay. I spent nearly a decade in hospitality, including fine dining, where every detail is noticed. I bring that same eye to every space I clean.

When you hire The Clean Standard, you work with me directly.
