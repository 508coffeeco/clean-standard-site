Janitorial Services in Tampa Bay

Tampa · St. Petersburg · Clearwater · Recurring nightly & weekly service

The Clean Standard provides recurring janitorial service for Tampa Bay businesses: the routine cleaning that keeps an office, practice, or facility ready every morning. We clean nightly or a few times a week, after hours, following a written checklist built for your space. The owner is on every account.

Every account starts with a free walkthrough and a quote within 24 hours. Service is month-to-month, and new recurring accounts get a free trial clean.

Janitorial vs. commercial cleaning

The terms overlap. "Janitorial" usually means the recurring, routine work: trash, restrooms, floors, and breakrooms on a set schedule. "Commercial cleaning" also covers one-time and deeper work like resets and post-event cleanups. We do both, and most businesses start with a recurring janitorial schedule and add a deep reset every few months.

What recurring janitorial service includes

  • High-touch surface disinfection: door handles, light switches, shared equipment
  • Restroom sanitation and restocking
  • Trash removal and liner replacement
  • Floor care: vacuuming, mopping, and spot treatment
  • Breakroom and kitchen cleaning
  • Entryway and reception detailing
  • Dusting of desks, surfaces, and common areas
  • Interior glass cleaning (reachable)
  • Conference room resets

Supply restocking (paper products and soap) is available on request.

How often?

Most small offices do well with one to three visits a week. Client-facing offices, medical practices, and coworking spaces usually need nightly service. Our guide on how often an office should be cleaned breaks it down, and a walkthrough gives you a schedule built around your actual space.
