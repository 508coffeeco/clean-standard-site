FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

How do you price commercial cleaning?

After a free walkthrough, we quote a flat per-visit price based on square footage, the number of restrooms, foot traffic, and how often you need us. You'll know the exact monthly cost up front.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Commercial service is month-to-month. We ask for 14 days' notice if you want to cancel.

When do you clean?

Whenever works for your business: before open, after close, overnight, or weekends.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes. We bring professional-grade supplies and equipment. If you have products you prefer, or need low-chemical options, just tell us.

Do you restock paper products and soap?

We can. We'll refill from your stock, or supply and invoice them at cost.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a week of your walkthrough.
